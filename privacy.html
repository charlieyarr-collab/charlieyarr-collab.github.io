MorcPoster – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

1. Who we are

MorcPoster is a desktop publishing tool operated by the owner of the M'Orc Next Door channel. It has exactly one user: the channel owner.

2. What data the Tool handles

  • TikTok account data – when the owner connects their TikTok account, TikTok provides an access token, the account's open ID, display name, username and avatar URL. These are used only to show which account will receive a post and to publish on that account.
  • Video files and captions – chosen by the owner on their own computer and sent to TikTok only when the owner presses Publish.
  • Post status – the publish ID and processing status returned by TikTok, kept in a local log file.

3. Where data is stored

Everything is stored locally on the owner's own computer. Access tokens are kept in a local file readable only by the owner's Windows account. Nothing is stored on any server operated by us, and nothing is shared with third parties other than TikTok itself.

4. Data about other people

The Tool collects no data about viewers, followers, commenters or any other TikTok user. It does not read other people's content, messages or profiles.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens stay until the owner disconnects the account or deletes the token file. Log files can be deleted at any time. Revoking MorcPoster's access from TikTok's "Manage app permissions" page immediately stops all access.

6. Cookies and tracking

This website and the Tool set no cookies and use no analytics or tracking.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the current version.
