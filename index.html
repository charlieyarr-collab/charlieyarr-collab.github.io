MorcPoster

A small desktop publishing tool for the M'Orc Next Door channel (@myorcnextdoor).

What it does

MorcPoster is a single-user tool. The channel owner picks a finished video on their own computer, writes the caption, chooses who can see the video, and publishes it to their own TikTok account. Nothing is uploaded until the owner presses Publish.

  • Shows the connected TikTok account before posting
  • Caption, audience, comment / Duet / Stitch and content disclosure are chosen by hand for every post
  • Videos are AI-generated and are labelled as such

Who uses it

Only the owner of the M'Orc Next Door channel. It is not offered to the public and stores no data about anyone else.
